About Us

A healing journey
in Famagusta

Famagusta Psychology Centre was founded to provide individuals, couples and families with evidence-based therapy services alongside licensed therapists, at the heart of North Cyprus.

Why we exist

Our centre was founded to fill a gap when access to psychological support services in North Cyprus was limited. With therapists trained to international standards, we provide an accessible, safe and ethical therapy environment both in Famagusta and online.

We offer sessions in both Turkish and English for international students at EMU (Eastern Mediterranean University) and other universities, expats living in Cyprus, and the local community.

Our belief is simple: therapy is not a luxury — it is a fundamental health right.

Our Values

What we are built on

🔒

Confidentiality

Everything shared is protected within the framework of professional ethics. Your sessions remain solely between you and your therapist.

💛

Non-Judgmental Space

Every reason for coming, every life story and every emotion is equally valued. We welcome you exactly as you are.

📋

Evidence-Based Practice

We apply only scientifically validated therapy methods: EMDR, CBT, psychodynamic therapy and more.

🌍

Cultural Awareness

We understand Cyprus's multicultural fabric and offer a sensitive approach to clients from diverse backgrounds.

🤝

Collaborative Process

We build therapy together with you. Your goals, your pace and your needs shape the process.

🌱

Continuous Development

Our therapists maintain professional growth through regular supervision and training. We keep learning for your wellbeing.
