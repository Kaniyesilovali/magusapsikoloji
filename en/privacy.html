8. Your Rights under GDPR

If you reside in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under GDPR Articles 15–22:

Right of Access (Art. 15)

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

You may request deletion of your data under certain conditions.

Right to Portability (Art. 20)

You may receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Object (Art. 21)

You may object to processing based on legitimate interest or for marketing purposes.

Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent for any processing based on your consent at any time.

To exercise your rights, email us at info@magusapsikoloji.com. Requests will be answered within 30 days.
