Data Protection Notice

Under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data

Last updated: 26 May 2025

As Famagusta Psychology Centre, we have prepared this notice to inform you about the processing of your personal data in accordance with Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("PDPL").

1. Data Controller

Business Name: Famagusta Psychology Centre

Address: Famagusta, North Cyprus

Email: info@magusapsikoloji.com

2. Categories of Personal Data Processed

The following categories of personal data may be processed in the course of our activities:

  • Identity data: First name, last name.
  • Contact data: Phone number, email address.
  • Health data (special category): Psychological assessment notes and therapy records — processed only with explicit consent.
  • Transaction security data: Website access logs, IP address.

3. Purposes of Processing

  • Scheduling appointments and managing the therapy process
  • Communicating with clients
  • Fulfilling legal obligations
  • Ensuring website security and technical operation
  • Statistics and reporting (only with your consent)

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Your personal data is processed on the following legal bases under PDPL Articles 5 and 6:

  • Explicit consent (particularly for health data)
  • Formation or performance of a contract
  • Fulfilment of a legal obligation by the data controller
  • Legitimate interest (website security)

5. Transfers of Personal Data

Your personal data will not be transferred to third parties except where required by law, court order or a request from a competent public authority. International transfers are only carried out with the safeguards set out in PDPL Article 9.

6. Methods of Data Collection

Your personal data is collected through the following means:

  • Messages sent via WhatsApp or email
  • During in-person or online counselling sessions
  • Technical access to this website

7. Retention Periods

Data Category Retention Period
Website access logs Maximum 30 days
Contact and appointment records Maximum 3 years after the relationship ends
Therapy and health records As required by law (maximum 10 years)

8. Your Rights under the PDPL

Under PDPL Article 11, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Be Informed

You may learn whether your personal data is being processed.

Right of Access

You may access your processed data and related information.

Right to Rectification

You may request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data.

Right to Erasure / Destruction

Where conditions are met, you may request deletion of your data.

Right to Object

You may object to outcomes arising from automated processing that are detrimental to you.

Right to Compensation

You may request compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing.

9. How to Submit a Request

To exercise your rights under the PDPL, please contact us through the following channel:

Email: info@magusapsikoloji.com

Requests will be answered within 30 days. Documents verifying your identity must be included with your application.

10. Complaint to the Personal Data Protection Authority

If your request is rejected, the response is deemed insufficient, or no response is received within 30 days, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Authority (PDPA) of Türkiye.

Authority website: www.kvkk.gov.tr

For Users in the European Union

If you are visiting from an EU/EEA country, please also review our Privacy Policy for your rights under GDPR.
