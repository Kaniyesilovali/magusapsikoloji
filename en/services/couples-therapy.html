Couples Therapy

Every relationship goes through difficult periods. Couples therapy is a safe space where both partners can be heard and work together towards a healthier relationship.

Free Discovery Call

This therapy can help with:

  • Communication breakdown and conflict
  • Trust issues and infidelity recovery
  • Recurring arguments and emotional distance
  • Intimacy and sexual difficulties
  • Major transitions (new baby, relocation, loss)
  • Deciding about the future of the relationship

Our approach to couples therapy

A neutral, safe space

The therapist is not on anyone's side. Both partners are heard equally and the aim is to understand the relationship, not to judge.

Communication tools

We develop practical techniques for couples to communicate more effectively, especially in difficult moments.

Understanding patterns

We explore the recurring cycles and underlying dynamics that keep couples stuck.

Flexible format

Couples therapy can be conducted in-person at our office or online — whatever suits both partners best.
