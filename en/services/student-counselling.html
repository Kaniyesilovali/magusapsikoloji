Student Counselling

Moving to a new country for university is exciting — but also challenging. We specialise in supporting international students and young adults through the unique pressures of student life in North Cyprus.

Free Discovery Call

We can help students with:

  • Academic stress, exam anxiety and performance pressure
  • Culture shock and adjustment to life abroad
  • Loneliness, homesickness and social isolation
  • Identity, self-esteem and life direction
  • Relationship difficulties and breakups
  • Anxiety, depression and burnout

Why students choose us

🌍

English-language sessions

All our student counselling sessions are conducted in English, making it easier to express yourself fully and be truly understood.

💻

Online option available

Fit therapy around your lecture schedule. Online sessions mean you can attend from your dorm room or anywhere with an internet connection.

🔒

Private and confidential

Everything you share stays between you and your therapist. Your university or family will not be informed — unless you choose to tell them.

🤝

Culturally sensitive

Our therapists understand the experience of living far from home in a culturally different environment — a reality shared by thousands of students in North Cyprus.

🏛️

Studying at EMU or another university in North Cyprus?

Eastern Mediterranean University (EMU) has one of the most internationally diverse student bodies in the region. We regularly work with students from across Africa, the Middle East, Asia and Europe who are navigating the challenges of studying abroad. You're not alone — and help is close by.

How to get started

1

Message us

Drop us a WhatsApp message or email — a single line is enough to get started.

2

Free call

We'll take a few minutes to understand what you need and match you with the right therapist.

3

Begin sessions

In-person in Famagusta or online — at a time that fits around your classes.

You don't have to struggle alone

Reaching out is the hardest part — we'll take care of the rest. Your first call is free.
