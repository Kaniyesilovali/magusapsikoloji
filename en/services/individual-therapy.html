Services / Individual Therapy

Individual Therapy

We work together in a safe, one-to-one space to help you better understand yourself, break destructive patterns and live a more fulfilling life.

Free Discovery Call

This therapy can help with:

  • Anxiety, panic and chronic stress
  • Depression and emotional burnout
  • Low self-esteem and self-sabotage
  • Loss, grief and major life changes
  • Relationship difficulties and loneliness
  • Personal growth and identity exploration

How do we work?

Initial Assessment

In the first session we listen to you and your reason for coming. This session carries no commitment; simply share what you feel ready to.

Personal Plan

We create a plan from CBT, psychodynamic therapy or integrative approaches based on your needs.

Regular Sessions

We typically progress with 50-minute sessions once a week. Each session builds on the last.

Flexible Format

In-person at our Famagusta office or online sessions via secure video platform are both available.
