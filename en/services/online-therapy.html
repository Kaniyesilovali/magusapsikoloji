Online Therapy

Geography should never be a barrier to good mental health care. Our online therapy service connects you with our licensed therapists from anywhere in the world via a secure video platform.

Book Online Session

Online therapy is ideal if you:

  • Live outside of Famagusta or North Cyprus
  • Have a busy schedule with limited flexibility
  • Prefer the comfort and privacy of your own home
  • Are a student or expat looking for English-language therapy
  • Have mobility challenges or health concerns

How online therapy works

💬

1. Get in touch

Message us on WhatsApp or email to arrange your free discovery call.

📅

2. Book your session

We'll match you with a therapist and find a time that works for you. You'll receive a secure video link.

🖥️

3. Connect and begin

Join from any device. All you need is a stable internet connection, a camera and a private space.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person?

Research consistently shows that online therapy is comparably effective to face-to-face sessions for most conditions, including anxiety, depression, trauma and relationship issues. CBT, talk therapy and even EMDR have all been successfully adapted for online delivery. The most important factor is the therapeutic relationship — and that thrives online too.
