Child Psychology

Children experience the world differently. Our child psychologists use age-appropriate, play-based and creative approaches to support children's emotional health and development.

Free Discovery Call

We can help children with:

  • Anxiety and fears
  • Behavioural difficulties and aggression
  • School adjustment and academic stress
  • Grief, loss and family changes (divorce, relocation)
  • Social difficulties and peer relationships
  • Trauma and emotional dysregulation

Our approach to working with children

Play-based therapy

Children naturally express themselves through play. We use games, art and stories to create a safe, comfortable therapeutic space.

Parent involvement

Parents are an essential part of their child's wellbeing. We provide guidance to parents to support progress at home.

Age-appropriate methods

Our therapists adapt their approach to suit the child's developmental stage, from early childhood through adolescence.

School liaison (if needed)

With parental consent, we can liaise with schools to coordinate support for children facing academic or social difficulties.
