Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

CBT is one of the most thoroughly researched and effective forms of psychotherapy. It works by exploring the links between thoughts, emotions and behaviours — and changing the patterns that keep us stuck.

Free Discovery Call

CBT is highly effective for:

  • Anxiety disorders and panic attacks
  • Depression and low mood
  • OCD and intrusive thoughts
  • Social anxiety and phobias
  • Insomnia and sleep problems
  • Perfectionism and self-criticism

How does CBT work?

CBT is based on the idea that our thoughts, emotions, physical sensations and behaviours are all interconnected. Negative thought patterns can create a vicious cycle that perpetuates distress. By identifying and challenging these patterns, we can break the cycle and develop healthier ways of thinking and behaving. CBT is typically structured, goal-focused and time-limited — often 8 to 16 sessions.
