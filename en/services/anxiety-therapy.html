Anxiety Therapy

Anxiety is one of the most common challenges affecting daily life. With evidence-based approaches, we work together to understand, manage and overcome anxiety.

Free Discovery Call

This therapy can help with:

  • Generalised anxiety disorder (GAD)
  • Panic attacks and panic disorder
  • Social anxiety
  • Phobias and obsessive thoughts
  • Chronic stress and burnout
  • Performance anxiety and perfectionism

Approaches we use

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

Identifying and restructuring thought patterns that fuel anxiety. Highly effective for panic disorder, social anxiety and phobias.

EMDR

Particularly effective when anxiety is rooted in past traumatic experiences.

Mindfulness-Based Techniques

Conscious awareness practices to help reduce the physical and emotional symptoms of anxiety.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Learning to relate to anxious thoughts differently — accepting without being consumed by them.
