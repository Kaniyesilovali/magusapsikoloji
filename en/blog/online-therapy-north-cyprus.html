Online Therapy in North Cyprus: How It Works and Who It's For

Online therapy has moved from a pandemic-era compromise to a preferred option for many people. For residents of North Cyprus — whether you are a student, an expat, or someone living outside Famagusta — it removes the most common barriers to getting support.

The evidence on online therapy is now substantial. Multiple large-scale studies and meta-analyses have confirmed that video-based therapy produces outcomes equivalent to face-to-face sessions for anxiety, depression, PTSD and most common psychological difficulties. It is not a lesser option — it is a different format with its own genuine advantages.

How does online therapy work in practice?

A typical online therapy session at Mağusa Psikoloji Centre works like this:

1

You book via WhatsApp

You message us to arrange a time. We confirm the appointment and send a secure video link — no app download required, just a browser.

2

You connect from a private space

At the session time, you join from wherever you are — your room, your car, a quiet café corner. The key is privacy and a stable connection. Headphones help significantly.

3

The session runs exactly like in-person therapy

50–60 minutes. The therapist listens, reflects, challenges and guides — the same process as a physical session. Many clients forget they are not in the same room within a few minutes.

4

Follow-up between sessions

Any exercises, reflections or resources from the session can be shared via a secure message. The continuity between sessions is the same as with in-person work.

Online therapy is not second-best — for many people it works better

Some clients find it easier to open up on video than in a clinical room. The home environment feels safer; there is no travel anxiety before a session; and clients in rural parts of North Cyprus can access the same quality of support as those in Famagusta city centre. The research consistently supports these observations.

Who is online therapy best suited for in North Cyprus?

International students and university residents

Living on or near campus, with unpredictable schedules and no private transport, online sessions eliminate the commute and fit around lectures, exams and part-time work.

Expats and long-term residents

Accessing English-language therapy in a small territory is already difficult. Online sessions open up the option of working with a qualified English-speaking therapist in Famagusta regardless of where in TRNC you live.

People with busy or shift-based schedules

Without the 20–30 minutes of travel time each way, online therapy is easier to fit into demanding working weeks. Early morning, lunchtime and evening slots become practical in a way they are not with in-person sessions.

Those with social anxiety or health concerns

For people whose anxiety makes public spaces difficult, or who have physical health conditions affecting travel, online therapy removes a significant practical barrier to starting.

Privacy and confidentiality in online sessions

We use end-to-end encrypted video platforms for all online sessions. Sessions are never recorded. No information about your attendance or content is shared with any third party, including your university, employer or family, except in the narrow legal circumstances that apply to all therapy.

If you are concerned about privacy at your end — for instance, a shared student flat — using headphones and taking the session from a private space (car, library study room, outdoor setting) is usually sufficient.

Getting started: booking your first online session

  • Step 1: Free discovery call

    Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a free 20-minute discovery call. No commitment. This lets us understand what you are looking for and answer any questions about the process.

  • Step 2: Book your first session

    If the discovery call feels right, we schedule a first full session. We confirm your preferred video platform and send the secure link in advance.

  • Step 3: Join from anywhere in TRNC

    Your room, your office, your car — wherever gives you 50 minutes of privacy. Stable internet and headphones are all you need.

When is in-person therapy recommended instead?

For most concerns, online therapy is equally effective. In-person sessions may be preferable for certain trauma-processing techniques (like EMDR), for clients who find technology frustrating, or for those who value the ritual of leaving home for a session. We offer both formats — and in some cases, a mix of the two.
