Our Approach

At the heart of therapy
is human connection

Techniques are tools; real change happens within a trusting, genuine relationship. This belief underpins every session we deliver.

Person-centred practice

Every individual is unique. The same diagnosis can create entirely different experiences in two different people. That's why instead of standard "prescriptions," we offer a process developed together with each client.

A therapist may be an expert; but the only expert on your own life is you. We simply accompany you in discovering that expertise.

"No two clients are the same — and neither are any two sessions."
"Being evidence-based means following new research and bringing it into practice."

Science and intuition together

We apply scientifically validated methods such as EMDR, CBT, psychodynamic therapy and ACT. But we use these methods flexibly according to each client's needs — we don't mechanically apply rigid protocols.

Balancing both the research agenda and clinical intuition is the key to effective therapy.

Trauma-informed care

Trying to "change" someone without first understanding why they behave as they do doesn't work. Together, we understand the experiences, wounds and defences behind behaviours.

A safe therapeutic relationship is a prerequisite for working through trauma.

"There is always meaning behind behaviour."

The approaches we use

Not one model, but multiple frameworks integrated according to each client's needs.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

A structured, goal-focused approach for recognising thought patterns and replacing them with more functional ones.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing — a powerful tool for reintegrating traumatic memories.

Psychodynamic Therapy

An in-depth approach that explores unconscious processes and early relationship patterns to create lasting change.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

An approach centred on accepting rather than suppressing emotions and living in line with personal values.

Systemic Therapy

A framework that views the individual within their family, relationships and environment; commonly used in family and couples therapy.

Mindfulness-Based Approaches

Techniques that strengthen emotional regulation and reduce stress and anxiety through conscious awareness practice.
